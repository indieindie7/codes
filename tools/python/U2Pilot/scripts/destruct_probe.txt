# destruct_probe: can a map's placed props be replaced at runtime (Black-style destruction)?
# Needs U2Destruct.u in System (EditPackages=U2Destruct, UCC make). The probe turns the view
# to the nearest solid prop, then every 2 s: hide + collision off, a solid copy in its
# place, Karma debris from the same mesh. Results: "DestructProbe:" lines in Unreal2.log.
map M08A1
waitcontrol 180
wait 2
console BeMyMonkey
console god
spawn U2Destruct.DestructProbe 200
wait 1
shot
# stage 2: original hidden, collision off
wait 2
shot
# stage 3: the copy in its place
wait 2
shot
# stage 4: debris dropped, then 4 s to settle
wait 1
shot
wait 4
shot
quit

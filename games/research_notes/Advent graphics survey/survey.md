# Advent Rising: graphics and optimization survey (2026-10-08)

A shortlist, not an essay. Targets: our d3d8to9 fork (`d3d8to9-gi`, U2Shaders layer) and AdventMod's UnrealScript.
Fit = how well it fits our wrapper. Effort: S = days, M = a week or two, L = more.

## What we already have (confirmed in source)

`u2shaders.hpp` ParseLine handles: `shader= layer= decal= gloss=(glossfx/glossenv/glossdry/glossreflect) surface= glass= replace= texgrade= tint= pbr= charlight= relight= shadowtint= pcss=(pcssparams/pcssdebug/pcssprobe) post=(bloom/grade/colour/sharpen/lut/postfx/postsplit/postdebug) colorblind= lens= streaks= strings= bloodrun/bloodlive hands= lmcapture= sketch= gmpanel/gmterrain live= psreplace=`, plus a second parser (line ~8890) for the GI/SSAO/SSS/SMAA/MSAA settings. AdventMod README adds in-game rows for post presets, soft shadows, shadows for 20 characters, SMAA, frame cap, VSync, trilinear, widescreen, FOV, colorblind.

**Checked locally:** `H:\SteamLibrary\steamapps\common\Advent Rising\System\advent.exe` PE characteristics = `0x10F`: **no Large Address Aware flag** (Unreal II has it). `default.ini` has `LevelOfAnisotropy=4`, `ReduceMouseLag=False`, `UseTripleBuffering=False`, `UseVSync=False`, `DetailTextures=True/False` per section; no `CacheSizeMegs` line.

## 2. Techniques: look per effort

| Technique | Notes | Evidence | Fit | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Specular/normal via replacement | Already covered by `pbr=`/`surface=`/`replace=`. The better version is authored material maps for the 20 most-seen world textures (not AI-generated normals everywhere). | - | High | M (art) |
| Parallax | Already in `surface=`/`decal=`. Nothing better is worth it on UE2 geometry (POM self-shadowing is the next step). | - | - | - |
| Sun shadows: cascaded / VSM | We have PCSS on the game's projector shadows. A real sun CSM needs a second scene pass of world geometry from the light: the wrapper would have to re-submit captured draws. EVSM would soften without PCSS's many taps. | [GPU Gems 3 ch.10 PSSM](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems3/part-ii-light-and-shadows/chapter-10-parallel-split-shadow-maps-programmable-gpus) | Low-Med | L |
| Light shafts (screen-space) | Mitchell's radial-blur god rays from depth-masked sky/sun; cheap, half-res, one pass. Advent has big skies and the sun position is known from the sky draw or a level `SunLight`. Fails when the sun is behind the camera (fade out). | [GPU Gems 3 ch.13](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems3/part-ii-light-and-shadows/chapter-13-volumetric-light-scattering-post-process), [Crysis sunbeams thread](https://community.khronos.org/t/sunbeams-in-crysis/54219) | High (we already have depth + projection in c4..c7) | S |
| Height/depth fog upgrade | Replace UE2 linear distance fog with exponential height fog in post, from depth; pairs with light shafts. | (standard technique) | High | S |
| TAA without motion vectors | Camera-only reprojection: depth + inverse view-proj (wrapper sees `SetTransform(VIEW/PROJECTION)`), neighbourhood clamp. Ghosts on moving characters unless they are masked (we already tag lit solid draws in `charlight`/`pbr`: use that mask to reject history). Also gives free temporal accumulation for GI/SSAO noise. | [luma.gl camera-reprojection AA](https://luma.gl/docs/api-reference/shadertools/shader-passes/camera-reprojection-antialiasing), [EEVEE commit](https://projects.blender.org/archive/blender-archive/commit/73d2e6f2021e649a1bf8952c37ce870562a884e4) | Med-High | M |
| MSAA via D3D9 | Already attempted; DXVK path + resolve before our post is the realistic route. | - | - | - |
| Forced 16x anisotropic | `default.ini` has `LevelOfAnisotropy=4`. Forcing `D3DSAMP_MAXANISOTROPY=16` + `MINFILTER=ANISOTROPIC` in the wrapper's SetTextureStageState translation (skip lightmap/point-sampled stages) is near free on modern GPUs. | [Render states D3D9](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/direct3d9/render-states) | High | S |

## 3. Optimizations

| Item | Notes | Evidence | Fit | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Large Address Aware on `advent.exe` | Checked: **not set** (`0x10F`). With our extra render targets, GI buffers, `replace=` DDS files and ImGui in a 32-bit process, the 2 GB ceiling is the most likely future crash. Flip bit 0x20 (keep a backup; Steam may restore the exe on verify). | [LAA explainer](https://bugnet.io/blog/how-to-fix-out-of-memory-crashes-in-32-bit-games-and-the-largeaddressaware-flag) | High | S |
| Redundant-state filtering | Cache last SetRenderState/SetTextureStageState/SetSamplerState/SetTexture/SetStreamSource values in the wrapper and drop repeats. The D3D9 runtime only filters on non-pure devices and misses A-B-A flips; the gain is CPU-side and matters for single-threaded UE2. Measure first. | [Pure devices (MS)](https://learn.microsoft.com/windows-hardware/drivers/display/pure-devices), [gamedev thread](https://gamedev.net/forums/topic/397317-state-change-guarding/) | High | S-M |
| Remove CPU/GPU sync points | Any `GetRenderTargetData`/`LockRect` read the same frame stalls the pipeline (GI depth, PCSS probe, pick passes, `charprobe`). Use a ring of 2-3 system-memory surfaces and read the one from N-2 frames; issue `D3DQUERYTYPE_EVENT` to know when it is ready. | [Accurately Profiling Direct3D API Calls](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/direct3d9/accurately-profiling-direct3d-api-calls) | High | S |
| DXVK settings | If shipping DXVK as an option: `dxvk.conf` with `d3d9.maxFrameLatency=1`, a frame limiter (`dxvk.framePace`/`d3d9.maxFrameRate`), and the GPL-async fork for shader-compile stutter (our ps compiles at runtime too). Note upstream disables NV low-latency in 32-bit apps. | [dxvk.conf (upstream)](https://sources.debian.org/src/dxvk/2.7.1-3/dxvk.conf), [GPLALL options guide](https://github.com/Digger1955/dxvk-gplall/wiki/dxvk.conf-Options-Guide) | Med | S |
| Frame pacing | We already have a frame cap; make it a waitable-timer limiter in the wrapper's `Present` (sleep + short spin) rather than UE2's `MaxFps`, and set `PresentationInterval` per VSync row. | (as above) | Med | S |
| Precompile our HLSL | Compile all rule shaders at load, not on first draw (removes first-sight hitches); cache bytecode on disk keyed by file hash. | - | High | S |
| UE2 ini tweaks | `CacheSizeMegs` (UT2004 `[Engine.GameEngine]`, community raises 32 to 128-800; helps loads, sometimes stutter), `ReduceMouseLag=False` (fps gain, it forces a GPU flush), `UseTripleBuffering=True`. Evidence is old forum lore, and `CacheSizeMegs` is absent from Advent's ini: check the exe strings before relying on it. | [AnandTech](https://forums.anandtech.com/threads/still-having-issues-with-ut-2k4.1353986/post-11899203), [Hardwareluxx](https://www.hardwareluxx.de/community/threads/ut2004-maus-lag.52556/latest), [Tom's](https://forums.tomshardware.com/threads/ut2003.171967) | Med | S |

## Recommended top 6 (in order)

1. **LAA flag on advent.exe**: checked missing; one bit, prevents out-of-memory crashes as our layer grows. S.
2. **Remove readback stalls** (N-2 ring buffers plus event queries for GI depth, PCSS and pick passes): likely the biggest frame-time win from our own effects. S.
3. **Forced 16x AF plus redundant-state filtering** in the wrapper: free sharpness at oblique angles plus CPU headroom for single-threaded UE2. S-M.
4. **Screen-space light shafts plus exponential height fog**: the most visible "remaster" look for Advent's big skies, from data we already have (depth, projection). S.
5. **Soft particles plus FP16 filmic tonemap plus grain/CA from prod80/SweetFX (MIT)**: cheap mood, fits the existing `postfx=` presets. S.
6. **Camera-reprojection TAA with a character mask**: better AA than SMAA 1x on foliage and wires, and it also denoises GI/SSAO temporally. M.

Not recommended now: RTX Remix (replaces our pipeline, NVIDIA-only), sun CSM (needs scene re-submission), AI texture upscaling (user declined).

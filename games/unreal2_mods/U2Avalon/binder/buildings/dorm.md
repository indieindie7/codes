halls; its back door faces the dock path. Window strips on both long sides. Per bunk a photo, a scrip chit
and a padlock; Reyes's bunk faces the window toward the dead rig because it was the last one free.

and the barricade faces away from the halls, toward the drain. The smugglers' road runs under this square,
and when dorm_c's door was torn outward and the husks were found in its hall, the contractors did not ask
what had come up the culvert: they sealed the grate, built the wall, and have shot at anything that moved in
the quarantine since. They are company men in a company war and the player is not on their list.

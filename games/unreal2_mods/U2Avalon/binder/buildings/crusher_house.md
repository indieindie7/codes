Placed by the chain: just below the portal, the run-of-mine pad between them, the belt gallery leaving its
back wall for the transfer tower. 14 x 10 m and 12 m tall, a tall narrow shed over a jaw crusher and its
hopper (engineer s.1: [mine_portal + headframe] -> [crusher_house] -> ROM pad). Dust, not steam, over it.
Systems: it stands with the portal at the top of the ore order (one source, two buildings); the tools may
split it into its own stage once the belt is drawn.

The loudest building on the island when it runs and the quietest when it stops: the night line knows the
shift change by the crusher going silent before the whistle. Dudek has run it for eleven years and keeps a
jar of the first ore on the control panel, which nobody is allowed to move.

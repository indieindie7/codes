The hero (parti, approved 2026-10-08). Built in the boom on the island's summit, after the company let the
Authority keep the old survey tower because it was useless to them. A battered stepped plinth (30 m, 8-12 deg
batter, 3 terraces, each 0.7-0.8x the one below), a ceremonial stair cut into the mass on the axis that nobody
climbs, a truck road beside it that everybody uses, a setback machine house with the LIANDRI wordmark, rust-red
domes at three sizes, and two lattice jibs with counterweights that lift nothing any more and stay because they
look like work. The only stepped, sand-and-red-dome mass on the island: one of it, never copied.

Inside: the scrip exchange hall, top-lit concrete, a queue rail for a hundred people and nobody in it, the
windows shuttered "until further notice" (pa02, pa15). The player sees it through the glass door from the gate
stair and is never let in: the one room shown and not given. The company's lounge is its terrace on the steps.

It is lower than the Authority tower, measured from its own foot, and it still stands higher, because the
company owns the hill. Its comms come from the company mast beside it.

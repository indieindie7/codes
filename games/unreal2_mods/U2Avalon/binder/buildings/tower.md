The oldest building on the island, and the poorest. Old concrete, few lights. Built by the first charter
survey before Liandri came; the company let the Authority keep it because it was useless to them. Runs on
company power through the one pylon line.

Measured from its own foot it is still the tallest; Liandri House on the summit stands higher only because
the company owns the hill. Not the hero any more (parti 2026-10-08): it is the player's home, the camera and
the dark frame. A vertical stalk with no batter, never the same shape as the company's stepped mass. Rooms:
the command room, the tower mess and bunks, the catwalk (binder/rooms/).

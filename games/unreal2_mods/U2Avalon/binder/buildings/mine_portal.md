The top of the ore line: placed by the chain (anchors/layout put it on the high inland ground above the
crusher, the straight belt falling from here to the silos). The portal is an arch cut into the hill with a
headframe over it; until the parts kit has a headframe, kind mast stands the lattice tower in its place.
8 x 8 m at the foot, 24 m to the sheave wheel (engineer s.1: mine, high, inland, z highest).

The first ore on the island came out of this hole, before the rigs and before the company learned the
sea-floor paid better. The mine crews go in at six with masks that do not fit and come out with the hill
on their skin. The whistle on the water tower is set to this portal's shift, not the plant's.

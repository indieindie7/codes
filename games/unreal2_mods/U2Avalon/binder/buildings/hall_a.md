The first hall, from the landing years, re-roled by the chain (D9, engineer s.1 and s.2.1): crushing and
grinding. The feeder belt from the silos enters by the right roller door at roof height; a crusher and its
hopper at the high end, two mills along the hall under a hoist beam, the cyclone cluster on a 4 m platform;
the slurry line leaves the low end for hall_b. 36 x 16 x 10 m so that two mills fit. The hands come in from
the dorm by the left personnel door. Patched panels, the oldest sawtooth roof, the painted line on the floor
from the right door that the director walks at five and nobody else steps on. The dead hall was the old mill;
this one carries on its job. The cooling towers no longer serve it: they serve the generator.

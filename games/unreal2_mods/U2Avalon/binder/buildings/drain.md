Underground: runs under the spine from the works plateau to an outfall below the pump house. A concrete vault
of pillars that takes the plant's runoff and the desalination brine to the sea under the town. A tide line on
every pillar, Arashiro's sample jars on a ledge (the stain darker jar by jar), Haldane's bucket and chalk mark
under the weep at kilometre three ("KM3, 2nd YR"). The company sign says NO ACCESS. MAINTENANCE MATTER (pa14).
Rook's boats tie up at its sea end, and his crates wait on a pallet above the waterline for the Tin Row traders:
it is how he reaches Tin Row and the dead hall without passing the checkpoint. The outlaws' road.

At the far end, one lamp that should not be lit. Its two doors are its two ends; lit: no is deliberate (it has
users, it is not abandoned).

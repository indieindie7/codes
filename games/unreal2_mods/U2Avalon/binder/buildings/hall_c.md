The second hall of the landing years, on the slope. Closed after the blowout took the old rig's supply;
never reopened, never pulled down. Roof panels gone, roller door jammed half up. Nobody goes near it.

Inside: eleven lockers with names stencilled, the eleven of the memorial, never emptied, and blowout-year
work orders on a clipboard. One locker is new: Rook keeps fuel cans in it, and a lamp burns in here that
should be off. His use is secret, so he is not on the users line, on purpose: the checker should not know
either. The drain opens into it at the junction.

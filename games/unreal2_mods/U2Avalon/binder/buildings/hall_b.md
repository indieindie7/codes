The boom-time hall, bigger and lit, re-roled by the chain (D9, engineer s.1 and s.2.1): flotation and
filters, one step down from hall_a. 48 x 20 x 14 m on two pads 4.8 m apart (pads: 2 step=4.8, for
terrain_cutfill once it reads the key): the upper floor at the uphill gable takes the slurry from hall_a and
the regrind mill; the lower floor holds three rows of flotation cells stepping down, rougher to cleaner, and
two filters over the concentrate bin at the low gable, where the roller door opens for the loader and the
slurry line leaves for the thickener and the tank farm. A mezzanine at +4.8 m along the yard wall looks down
on the froth; a high catwalk at +9.6 m ends at a door onto the gable platform, the dusk frame. The canteen
corner is at the left end where the personnel door is: a microwave on a pallet, a radio, a safety poster
with a child's drawing taped to it. The plant control room sits on the uphill gable (rooms: control_room).

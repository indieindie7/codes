On top of hall_b's uphill gable, on a stair tower, 14 x 8 m and 3.6 m high (D10, engineer s.2.3). The wall
that faces the plant is glass from a 0.6 m sill to 3.2 m, mullions every 2 m, the glass sloped outward 15
degrees against the glare: the classic control-room look, and a second window frame, onto the plant rather
than the tower's view. A raised floor one step up. Four consoles in a shallow arc 3 m back from the glass,
2.4 m wide each, three or four screens apiece, and the screens run left to right in the same order the
buildings stand through the glass: the generator sorts them by their bearing from this room, no mirror
imaging. Behind the consoles the mimic board on the back wall, an MCC closet, a kitchenette, two ways out:
the stair, and the bridge to the hall's high catwalk.

The consoles face the plant and never the tower: the company does not look up. Okafor-Strand stands here at
five with her hands behind her back; the plant staff sit. On the mimic board the dead hall's line is still
drawn, painted over once, the paint worn through.

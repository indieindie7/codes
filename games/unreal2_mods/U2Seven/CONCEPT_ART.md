# The Seven: concept art brief

Concept art for U2Seven, the story mod. Nothing here ships in the mod; it is
for posters, README headers, the intro/outro cards and for keeping the look
consistent. Prompts are written for Stable Diffusion style generators
(positive / negative), 16:9 unless noted. Swap the style tag at the top of
each prompt for whatever your model likes.

## The look

- **Mood:** slow-burn paranoia. Lonely, cold, procedural. Nobody runs.
- **Palette:** steel blue, dead grey, one warm source per frame (a lamp, a
  console, the artifacts' dull amber). Never saturated. Hawkins scenes: pure
  clean white light, no grain.
- **Grain rule:** every crew shot has film grain and radio-static texture.
  Hawkins has none. He is the only clean thing in the story. That is the tell.
- **Framing:** wide, small figures, big empty rooms. Faces lit from below by
  consoles. Cinematic 2003 sci-fi, not glossy.
- **The seven artifacts:** hand-sized, angular, looks carved not machined,
  dark grey-green stone with faint amber veins that brighten as more of them
  are together. They fit into each other. Seven together form a rough ring:
  "a key".

Style tag used below: `cinematic concept art, matte painting, muted palette,
film grain, 2000s sci-fi, wide shot, volumetric fog, painterly, detailed`

Shared negative: `text, watermark, logo, blurry, oversaturated, neon, anime,
cartoon, extra limbs, deformed hands, glossy, lens flare`

## Characters

Kept close to the game's cast, restyled for the mood.

**Dalton** (Marshal). Late 40s, ex-military, tired, close-cropped grey hair,
worn black-and-grey marshal coat over armour, no insignia. The empty chair is
his story: he is always slightly apart from the group.

> portrait of a weary space marshal, late forties, close-cropped grey hair,
> worn dark tactical coat over scuffed armour, no insignia, lit from below by
> a console, steel blue shadows, {style}
> Negative: {shared}, young, smiling, clean uniform, medals

**Aida** (XO, intelligence). 30s, sharp, blonde hair tied back, analyst's
headset, always holding a data slate. The one who counts what is wrong.

> portrait of a ship's intelligence officer, thirties, blonde hair tied back,
> headset, holding a data slate showing radio traffic logs, sceptical
> expression, dim bridge, one amber screen, {style}

**Ne'Ban** (pilot, alien). Tall thin alien in a heavy flight suit, long face,
large calm dark eyes, hands with long fingers on the controls. Serene, not
monstrous. The listener.

> tall slender alien pilot in a heavy grey flight suit, long calm face, large
> dark eyes, long fingers resting on cockpit controls, viewport full of
> stars, quiet, {style}
> Negative: {shared}, scary, teeth, slime, insect

**Isaak** (engineer). 50s, heavy build, beard, oil-stained coveralls, a wrench
he never puts down. Act III: sitting alone in the hold with the artifacts,
listening.

> bearded engineer in his fifties, heavy build, stained coveralls, sitting
> alone on the floor of a dim cargo hold beside seven glowing stone
> artifacts, head tilted, listening, single hanging work lamp, {style}

**Hawkins** (liaison to "Command"). Never seen in the game. When shown: a
clean, pleasant middle-aged man in a plain suit, alone in a white empty room
with one desk, one chair and one microphone. No windows. No grain.

> a pleasant middle-aged man in a plain grey suit sitting alone at a single
> desk in a vast empty white room, one microphone, no windows, perfectly
> clean, soft even white light, unsettling calm, {style but remove film grain
> and fog}
> Negative: {shared}, film grain, dirt, shadows, other people

**Dr Meyer** (Janus). Exhausted researcher, 40s, lab coat over cold-weather
gear, surrounded by notes, two artifacts on the bench. Seen only via a
found recording, so: frame it as a paused video, scan lines.

> exhausted female researcher in a lab coat over cold weather gear, cluttered
> research station bench, two dark stone artifacts with amber veins, walls of
> handwritten notes, seen through a paused video recording with scan lines,
> {style}

## Key frames, one per episode

Each is a poster candidate for that episode's title card.

1. **Sanctuary.** Colony landing pad at dusk, dropship engines still glowing,
   no people anywhere, doors open, lights on. "Three hundred people. Nothing
   moves."
   > deserted colony landing pad at dusk, a dropship with engines glowing,
   > every door open, every light on, no people, wind, steel blue and one
   > warm engine glow, {style}

2. **Marsh.** The Atlantis crashed on its side in a swamp, half in water,
   dawn fog, small figures holding a perimeter. Isaak on the hull.
   > a crashed starship lying on its side in a misty swamp, half submerged,
   > dawn fog, three small figures guarding a perimeter, one figure on the
   > hull with a torch, {style}

3. **Hell.** Frozen outpost, blizzard, Izarians carrying an artifact away in
   a line of torches, seen from far above.
   > frozen mining outpost in a blizzard, seen from a cliff above, a line of
   > small alien figures carrying torches across the ice, one carries a
   > faintly glowing stone, {style}

4. **Acheron.** The living ground: dark organic terrain that ripples, a lone
   marshal walking in, Ne'Ban's dropship refusing to land, hovering far back.
   > a lone armoured figure walking across dark organic alien ground that
   > ripples like breathing skin, a dropship hovering far behind refusing to
   > land, low green-grey light, {style}

5. **Janus.** Research station buried in snow, one window lit, aurora. Inside
   the window, a bench with two artifacts.
   > a research station buried in snow at night, one window lit warm amber,
   > aurora overhead, tiny, isolated, {style}

6. **Na Koja Abad.** Desert ruins, the crew's dropship landed, Ne'Ban standing
   beside it while Dalton argues into a radio. Distance between the two.
   > desert ruins at noon, harsh flat light, a landed dropship, a tall alien
   > pilot standing beside it, a marshal some distance away speaking into a
   > radio, empty space between them, {style}

7. **Drakk hive.** Inside the Atlantis cargo hold: six artifacts on the deck
   arranged in a partial ring, one gap, Isaak's silhouette sitting with them.
   > dim starship cargo hold, six dark stone artifacts on the deck forming a
   > broken ring with one gap, amber veins glowing, a seated silhouette
   > watching them, one hanging lamp, {style}

8. **Avalon.** The Atlantis docked at a military station, hold door open,
   empty. Isaak's wrench on the floor. Nobody.
   > a starship docked inside a vast military hangar, cargo ramp down, hold
   > empty and lit, a single wrench lying on the ramp, no people, {style}

9. **Dorian Gray.** The seven artifacts complete the ring on a white floor.
   Dalton alone. Hawkins's white room bleeding into the frame from one side:
   grain on the left, clean on the right.
   > a marshal standing alone before seven stone artifacts completing a
   > glowing amber ring on a white floor, the left half of the image grainy
   > and dark, the right half perfectly clean white light, {style}

10. **Epilogue.** Empty captain's chair on a dark bridge. Log light blinking.
    "Nothing to report."
    > an empty captain's chair on a dark starship bridge, one small recording
    > light blinking red, stars through the viewport, {style}

## Poster

Title: THE SEVEN. Vertical 2:3. The ring of seven artifacts as the "O" in
SEVEN is the obvious move; the alternative is the empty chair with the ring
on the seat.

> movie poster composition, a ring of seven dark stone artifacts glowing
> amber floating above an empty captain's chair on a dark bridge, small
> figures of four crew members below looking up, one figure standing apart,
> steel blue and amber, {style}, vertical

## Workflow notes

- Generate the artifacts first and reuse the result (img2img or a reference
  image) so all seven frames show the same object.
- Do the characters as portraits at 2:3, then drop them into the wide frames
  with inpainting rather than asking for both at once.
- The Hawkins frame is the only one where you turn grain and fog off. Keep it
  that way in every variant.

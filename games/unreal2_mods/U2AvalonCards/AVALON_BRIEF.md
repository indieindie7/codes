# Avalon (TutA) - place brief, plan B: "the tenant"

What the island has to say from Hawkins' window, before any mesh is placed. Every placed thing should
answer to this page; if it doesn't, it doesn't go in.

## Premise
Avalon is a company world. A mining corporation (working name **Izanagi**, whose Na Koja Abad kit we
reuse) pulls ore and oil from the sea floor here. The Terran Colonial Authority rents one old tower on the
company's island as its sector office. The company supplies the landing rights, the power and the water;
the Authority supplies "the law".

One line: **the Authority keeps the peace in a sector where the company owns the peace.**

That's why Dalton gets "the quietest patrol": nothing happens here that the company doesn't allow.

## What's wrong (the tension the view must show)
- The tower is the tallest building on the island and the poorest. Old concrete, few lights.
- The company's plant is new and busy, lit up, with traffic coming and going. Outside is busy; inside is quiet.
- The tower runs on company power: one cable line runs from the plant up to it. The company can
  switch the law off.
- The Authority has one small pad. The company has the harbour, the dock and the cargo traffic.

## Systems (Coruscant rule: a place is its systems)
| System | Where it is | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| Product | sea floor -> rigs -> pipeline -> plant -> tanks -> cargo pad -> off-world | a readable chain left to right in the window |
| Power | plant generators + cooling columns | one pylon/cable line to the tower |
| Water | desalination tanks at the harbour | tank row by the shore |
| Supply in | company cargo dropships at the harbour pad; the Authority's own small pad by the tower | two pads, very different sizes |
| Waste | flare stack on the rig, discoloured water by the plant outfall | smoke/flare, a stain in the sea |
| Time | an old rusted rig left standing, a new rig further out | history without words |

## Who lives here (two days, as in the Coruscant video)
- **Authority comms tech, tower:** watches a traffic board that shows only company flights. Eats in
  the tower mess, takes the one lift, has never been down to the harbour. Logs a complaint about a power cut
  each week; it's never answered.
- **Company rig hand:** a shuttle out at dawn, a twelve-hour shift on the rig, paid in company scrip,
  sleeps in the ocean-base dorm. Sees the Authority people as tourists in a tower.

## Micro-stories (one line per placed thing, the Mars-manor rule)
- **The Authority pad and dropship:** the only Authority hardware outside. The company charges it docking fees.
- **The old rig:** decommissioned after a blowout, never removed; cheaper to leave it.
- **The new rig:** where the money is now; a flare burns at its top.
- **The pipeline:** runs straight past the tower's foot, not around it. The company was here first.
- **The comm mast on the west hill:** the company's, taller than the Authority's antenna.
- **The tree clusters:** the one hill left green, because the plant director likes the view from the ocean base.
- **The cable to the tower:** see Power.

## The one frame
From the long window at sunset: the plant lit and working, the flare on the new rig, a cargo dropship
lifting off the harbour pad, the dead rig beyond. Hawkins' room behind you is dim. If that frame reads
"busy out there, nothing in here", the place works.

# RAGE mutant wall and ceiling navigation — and how to import it into Advent Rising

Research note, 2026-10-09. Target: AdventMod (UE2 build 2226, UnrealScript Bot/SquadAI, path nodes + ReachSpecs, no navmesh; ModMinds psychology, scored path-node queries, path-cost profiles, AdventNative DLL hooks with bone callbacks, hound packs with flank/skip/pin roles).

User's ask: "do some research how rage hopping mutants use the wall and ceiling for navigation, would love to import it here."

Reading guide: **[D]** = documented by the source cited. **[I]** = my inference from public code of related systems or from reviews. **[A]** = verified in Advent's exported script source (`Documents\AdventRising_src`, batch-exported with AdventUCC). Licences are given where code is involved. No leaked source was used: Doom 3 and Quake III are id's official GPL releases; idTech 5 / RAGE source is not public and was not sought.

---

## 0. The one-paragraph answer

Every "parkour enemy" in the survey is built the same way: **(1) a special link in the navigation data** (an AAS reachability, a nav-mesh off-mesh link, a UE2 `JumpDest`-style special path) that says "from here you can get to there by a non-walking move", **(2) a traversal move that owns the body for its duration** (root-motion or ballistic physics + an animation chosen to fit the distance/height, with the body re-oriented to the surface), and **(3) a decision rule that prefers the link** (cheaper travel time, or a role such as "flanker" that only accepts wall routes). The ceiling/wall "crawl" is the rarest part and is usually either scripted (fixed spline / animation) or physics with the gravity vector replaced by the surface normal. Advent already has pieces (1) and (2) in stock form: `JumpDest` special paths with `SuggestMovePreparation`, a native ballistic `DoJumpTo`, `PHYS_Spider` in the engine enum, and — the big find — **a complete Seeker wall-kick chain** (`TryLeapToWall → Leap → CheckWallJump → WallJumpBegin → WallJumpEnd → RequestLeapOffWall → LeapOffWall`) that is exactly the RAGE "hopper" move; it is just never planned as navigation and is not enabled for hounds.

## 1. RAGE (2011) mutants: what id built, documented vs inferred

### What is documented
- The mutants do crawl along ceilings and walls to reach the player, and squeeze through cracks. HotHardware review (Joel Hruska, Oct 2011): mutants "will climb along the ceiling to reach you", "scramble through cracks in a wall". https://hothardware.com/reviews/ids-rage-flawed-flat-but-occasionally-fun?page=2 **[D]**
- The iPad port Rage HD review (Hexus, 2010/11) describes enemies "perching themselves up on high balconies or crawling like spiders across walls to descend quickly on your position". https://hexus.net/gaming/reviews/apple/31341-app-review-rage-hd-ipad-2-optmised-v21/?page=2 **[D]**
- Game Informer's review (Oct 2011) praises an enemy that recognised "the quickest path ... isn't a winding path, but rather cutting the distance by jumping over fences" — i.e. jump reachabilities are in the route search, not scripted. https://gameinformer.com/games/rage/b/pc/archive/2011/10/03/the-father-of-fps-returns-triumphant **[D]**
- The Ghost bandit clan is the "acrobatic" human faction (vault railings, go up catwalks with hooked blades) — Game Informer dev-diary coverage, 28 Jul 2011. https://gameinformer.com/games/rage/b/ps3/archive/2011/07/28/do-you-know-your-enemies-you-will-after-watching-this-dev-diary.aspx **[D]**
- Tim Willits (Destructoid interview, 2011) on the design: "Where the game shines is in both our combat animations and AI system and how well they work together" — hit reactions, crawling to cover, etc. https://www.destructoid.com/?p=112038 **[D]**
- RAGE Tool Kit (id, Feb 2013, Steam) exists and ships idStudio; its public welcome page says nothing about AI navigation. https://icculus.org/~marco/notmine/id-dev/rage-toolkit **[D]** (The kit is 35 GB and is id's internal tooling; not downloaded for this note.)
- No GDC/QuakeCon talk specifically about RAGE's mutant traversal was found. Carmack's QuakeCon keynotes cover megatexture/idTech 5 rendering, not AI.

### What is inferred
- **Navigation lineage [I]:** idTech 4 (Doom 3) uses the AAS (Area Awareness System) by J.M.P. van Waveren, a direct descendant of the Quake III botlib AAS. idTech 5 is the next engine by the same team (van Waveren was still at id through RAGE) and DOOM 2016 is documented (80.lv / Tommy Thompson, 2018) as having re-used RAGE's cover system, so the RAGE AI stack is the Doom 3 one extended. The safe assumption: RAGE's mutants route over an AAS-style area graph with **reachabilities** (walk / step / barrier-jump / walk-off-ledge / jump / special), and the ceiling/wall crawling is **either a "special" reachability placed by designers (idStudio entity) or scripted entry/exit sequences**, not free-form surface locomotion. Nothing public shows idTech 5 computing wall/ceiling reachabilities automatically.
- **Animation side [I]:** RAGE's enemy motion is clearly animation-driven with runtime fitting (Doom 2016, same team: "imps can jump long or short distances using the same animation that is tweaked at runtime to suit" — 80.lv, 2018). The "hopper" mutants' wall-kick looks like: ballistic leap to a wall point → short wall-plant pose aligned to the wall normal → second ballistic leap at the player. This is the same shape as Advent's Seeker chain (section 3/4).
- **Honest limit:** the exact RAGE implementation (how the ceiling crawl entry points are authored, whether it is a special reachability with a dict of params like Doom 3's `idReachability_Special`, or a scripted anim path) is not documented anywhere public. Treat everything below from Doom 3 as "the public ancestor", not "RAGE code".

### The public ancestor: Doom 3 AAS (GPL)
Source: id-Software/DOOM-3 on GitHub, GPL v3 (DOOM-3-BFG also GPL v3). Quake III Arena botlib: id-Software/Quake-III-Arena, GPL v2. **Use as reference for ideas and algorithms; GPL code must not be pasted into the mod unless the mod is GPL (it is — user's standing rule: mods are GPL/share-alike — but Advent's engine headers are not, so keep it to ideas and re-implementation).**

Reachability types, exact names:
- Doom 3 `neo/tools/compilers/aas/AASFile.h`: travel flags `TFL_INVALID, TFL_WALK, TFL_CROUCH, TFL_WALKOFFLEDGE, TFL_BARRIERJUMP, TFL_JUMP, TFL_LADDER, TFL_SWIM, TFL_WATERJUMP, TFL_TELEPORT, TFL_ELEVATOR, TFL_FLY, TFL_SPECIAL, TFL_WATER, TFL_AIR`; classes `idReachability_Walk, _BarrierJump, _WaterJump, _WalkOffLedge, _Swim, _Fly, _Special` (the Special one carries an `idDict dict` — a free-form parameter bag, the natural home for a "wall crawl" link); area flags `AREA_FLOOR, AREA_GAP, AREA_LEDGE, AREA_LADDER, AREA_LIQUID, AREA_CROUCH, AREA_REACHABLE_WALK, AREA_REACHABLE_FLY`; settings `maxStepHeight, maxBarrierHeight, maxWaterJumpHeight, maxFallHeight, minFloorCos, allowSwimReachabilities, allowFlyReachabilities, tt_barrierJump, tt_startWalkOffLedge`. https://github.com/id-Software/DOOM-3/blob/master/neo/tools/compilers/aas/AASFile.h **[D]**
- Quake III `code/game/be_aas.h` adds `TFL_ROCKETJUMP, TFL_BFGJUMP, TFL_GRAPPLEHOOK, TFL_DOUBLEJUMP, TFL_RAMPJUMP, TFL_STRAFEJUMP, TFL_JUMPPAD, TFL_FUNCBOB, TFL_BRIDGE, TFL_DONOTENTER`; `TFL_DEFAULT` for bots = walk|crouch|barrierjump|jump|ladder|walkoffledge|swim|waterjump|teleport|elevator|air|water|jumppad|funcbob. https://github.com/id-Software/Quake-III-Arena/blob/master/code/game/be_aas.h **[D]**
- **Which of these are about walls/ceilings? None.** `TFL_LADDER`/`AREA_LADDER`/`FACE_LADDER` is the only vertical-surface travel in either engine (a ladder face is a brush face flagged in the editor; the AI climbs it as a straight segment). Everything else is a floor-to-floor jump/drop. Doom 3's wall-crawling imps in the intro cinematics are scripted animation, not navigation. **[I]**

How the generator decides a jump/drop link (Doom 3 `AASReach.cpp`, `Reachability_Step_Barrier_WaterJump_WalkOffLedge`): for each floor edge of area 1, build a vertical plane through it, keep area-2 floor edges lying within 0.2 units of that plane, project both onto the plane, take the horizontal overlap and height difference; `0 < dh < maxBarrierHeight` and not crouch-only → `BarrierJump` with fixed travel time `tt_barrierJump`; `dh < 0` and within `maxFallHeight` → vertical hull trace at the landing, must end in area 2 and not cross a cluster portal → `WalkOffLedge` with time `tt_startWalkOffLedge + |dh|·50/gravity`. https://github.com/id-Software/DOOM-3/blob/master/neo/tools/compilers/aas/AASReach.cpp **[D]** These three tests (edge overlap, height band, clear vertical hull) are the model for Advent's auto-placed links (section 4).

How the AI uses them (Doom 3 `AAS_pathing.cpp` `WalkPathToGoal`, `AAS_routing.cpp`, `AI.cpp`):
- Routing floods reachabilities filtered by the agent's `travelFlags` (`badTravelFlags = ~travelFlags; if (reach->travelType & badTravelFlags) skip`); ground monsters use `TFL_WALK|TFL_AIR`, flyers add `TFL_FLY`; `TFL_INVALID` is set dynamically on reachabilities by `SetObstacleState` (blocked doors etc.) and is filtered out the same way. Ledge areas get a +250 time penalty when the agent can't fly. **[D]**
- `WalkPathToGoal` walks the chain, shortcutting straight walks; **it stops at the first non-walk reachability**: `path.moveGoal = reach->start`, and `path.type = PATHTYPE_WALKOFFLEDGE | BARRIERJUMP | JUMP`, `path.secondaryGoal = reach->end`, `path.reachability = reach`. So the AI walks to the link's start point, then the move code/script performs the special move to `secondaryGoal`. **[D]** This "walk to the link start, then hand the body to a special move" is the universal pattern.
- `AI_pathing.cpp` `Ballistics()` is the closed-form launch-angle solve (quadratic `a = 4y²+4x², b = -4v²-4yg, c = g²`, two roots, `angle = atan2(...)`), `PredictTrajectory` tries the low arc then the high arc and sweeps the hull along 2–4 arc segments (`TestTrajectory`), rejecting arcs above `max_height`; `Event_GetJumpVelocity(pos, speed, max_height)` returns `dir*speed` for monster leaps (the imp's leap attack calls it from script). `AI_JUMP` is set by `Event_JumpFrame` from an animation frame command — i.e. **the leap launches on an anim frame, not at state entry**. https://github.com/id-Software/DOOM-3/blob/master/neo/game/ai/AI_pathing.cpp and AI_events.cpp **[D]**
- Modders extended AAS with new special reachabilities (elevators/transporters) by adding `idReachability_Special` links per entity and teaching `idAI` to handle them: dhewm3 modwiki "Extending the Area Awareness System". https://modwiki.dhewm3.org/Extending_the_Area_Awareness_System **[D]** (page returned 402 at fetch time; cited from the search excerpt.)

## 3. The animation side, and what UE2 build 2226 / Advent can do

### How wall-run / ceiling-crawl poses are done elsewhere [I, standard practice]
1. **Orient-to-surface of the whole body:** the character's up vector is replaced by the surface normal; the root (or the whole mesh transform) is rotated so that "down" = `-normal`; the locomotion animation plays unchanged (a run cycle on a wall is just a run cycle with a rotated root). Horizon/Doom do this with root-bone warping; UE4 wall-running tutorials do it with a tilted capsule/mesh and a custom gravity direction.
2. **IK to the surface:** feet/hands traced along `-normal`, two-bone IK (what Advent's native foot IK already does on floors).
3. **Transitions:** jump-to-wall and wall-to-jump are short authored clips with root motion; the planted pose is held for 1–3 frames while the next ballistic velocity is applied on a frame event (Doom 3's `AI_JUMP` frame command).
4. **Gravity flip for crawl:** physics mode with gravity along `-normal` (UE2's `PHYS_Spider` is exactly this), or no physics at all: move the actor along a spline/segment and set the rotation from the normal each tick.

### What Advent has [A]
- **`PHYS_Spider` exists** in Advent's `Engine/Classes/Actor.uc` `EPhysics` (index 9, between `PHYS_MovingBrush` and `PHYS_Trailer`), with `PHYS_Ladder`, `PHYS_RootMotion`, `PHYS_Riding`, `PHYS_Levitate`, `PHYS_Karma`. `Pawn.DoJump` handles it: `if (Physics == PHYS_Spider) Velocity = JumpZ * Floor;` — a jump from a wall launches along the wall normal (`Pawn.Floor` is the const surface normal the native physics maintains). The Unreal Wiki describes `PHYS_Spider` as "Similar to PHYS_Walking except it applies to an Actor against any surface... When the end of the surface is reached the Pawn will be set to PHYS_Falling... only works on one plane, it will not allow a Pawn to move across perpendicular walls." https://unrealarchive.org/wikis/unreal-wiki/Legacy:PhysicsType.html **[D]** Whether the 2226 native `physSpider` is fully implemented (UT2003/4 kept it but no stock content uses it; the mesh is NOT rotated by it — only `Floor` changes) must be tested in game (step 3 below). The body rotation would come from our bone callback / `SetRotation` anyway.
- **Ballistic leap is native and already solved:** `Pawn.DoJumpTo(vector Destination, optional float DesiredAngleRadians)` (native event), `Pawn.DoLeapAttackTo` wraps it, `Pawn.DoLedgeJump`, `Controller.EAdjustJump(BaseZ, XYSpeed)` (UT2004-style velocity to reach `MoveTarget`), `Pawn.bCanWalkOffLedges`, `bCanJump/bCanFly/bCanClimbLadders/bCanDoubleJump`, `JumpZ` (default 420).
- **The Seeker wall-kick chain is complete in stock script** (WarfareGame/Classes/Bot.uc, EonEngine/Classes/EonPawn.uc, EonCharacters/Classes/Seeker.uc):
  1. `Bot.TryLeapAttack`: in range and `FRand() < Ability.LeapAttackAbility` → with probability `Ability.WallJumpAbility` call `TryLeapToWall`.
  2. `TryLeapToWall`: aim ±45° yaw (8192 urot) off the enemy direction, +3000 pitch (~16°), at `DistanceToEnemy`; if `FastTrace` is **blocked** (there is a wall there) → `DoLeap` to that point (state `Leap` → `Pawn.DoJumpTo(Destination)`), else try the other side.
  3. While falling the native calls `EonPawn.CheckWallJump(normal)` (Seeker overrides: up to 4 wall jumps, `Velocity.Z < 30` or in a reaction anim, a trace from the cylinder base of `CollisionRadius + 20` toward the wall must hit) → `WallJumpBegin(norm)`: rejects `norm.z < -0.17 || norm.z > 0.5`, chooses `WallJump_L`/`WallJump_R`, sets `wallJumpNormal`.
  4. Anim end → `WallJumpEnd` → `Controller.RequestLeapOffWall(wallJumpNormal)`: `Destination` = enemy if within `LeapMinRange..LeapMaxRange` and visible, else enemy direction clamped to `LeapMaxRange`, else `WallNormal * RandRange(LeapMinRange, LeapMaxRange)`; state `LeapOffWall` → `DoLeapAttackTo` / `DoJumpTo` → `WaitForLanding` → `WhatToDoNext('LeapOffWall_Finished')`.
  5. Gate: `PhysicsVolume.bAllowAILeaping` (default True). Ability fields (already written by ModMinds' `Names` list): `LeapAbility, LeapMinRange, LeapMaxRange, RandomLeapAbility, LeapAttackAbility, LeapAttackOffset, LeapAttackMin/MaxRange, WallJumpAbility`.
  - **Hounds:** `SeekerDog.uc` / `SeekerDogNative.uc` contain no `WallJump`/`CheckWallJump` code; `EonPawn.CheckWallJump` is a bare declaration (returns false), so dogs leap but never kick off walls. That is the gap to fill.
- **Special path links exist in this build:** `Engine/Classes/JumpDest.uc` (native): `UpstreamPaths[8]` (ReachSpecs that end here), `NeededJump[8]`, `CalculatedGravityZ[8]`, `bSpecialForced=True`, `SpecialCost(Pawn, ReachSpec)` returns 100 if `JumpZ/gravity` suffices else 10 000 000, and `SuggestMovePreparation(Pawn)` performs `DoJump`: `MoveTarget = self; Velocity = EAdjustJump(0, GroundSpeed); SetPhysics(PHYS_Falling); Controller.SetFall()`. `NavigationPoint` exposes `event int SpecialCost(Pawn, ReachSpec)`, `event bool SuggestMovePreparation(Pawn)`, `bSpecialMove`, `bSpecialForced`, `bOneWayPath`, `bBlocked`, `bAirPathNode`, `ExtraCost/TransientCost/FearCost`, `ForcedPaths[4]/ProscribedPaths[4]` (tags, applied at editor path build), `MaxPathDistSq`. `ReachSpec` (native Object): `Distance, Start, End, CollisionRadius, CollisionHeight, reachFlags, MaxLandingVelocity, bPruned, bForced`. `NavigationPoint.PathList` is `const array<ReachSpec>` — read-only to script, **writable from AdventNative** (const is a compiler rule, not memory protection), and the native route search (`FindPathTo/Toward`, `RouteCache[16]`, `CurrentPath`, `NextRoutePath`) is what AI-MINDS-DESIGN already drives through `ExtraCost`.
- **Body orientation hooks** (research/native-animation-hooks.md): native orient-to-floor tilts `Hips` via `SetBoneDirection(..., Space 2)` when `bOrientToFloor/bWantsToOrientToFloor`; the K6+K9 bone-director callback (`SetWorldSpacerFunction`, runs inside the pose build in bone order, `MeshToWorld` callable) can rotate `Hips`/root to any normal; foot IK already traces floors. Wall/ceiling poses are therefore reachable without new animation data: rotate Hips to `-normal`, let the run cycle play, IK feet to the surface.
- `PHYS_Flying` is used by stock Bot code for scripted hops (`Pawn.SetPhysics(PHYS_Flying)` in Bot.uc 1500/2863) — a usable fallback for a fake crawl.

## 4. Import plan for Advent, in priority order

Effort: S = an afternoon, M = 1–2 days, L = a week. All script unless noted.

### (a) Wall-leap reachabilities — `ModLeapLink` (M)
Two routes, cheapest first:
1. **Script-side traversal step (recommended first).** A `ModLeapLink` actor (not a NavigationPoint, so the editor/graph is untouched) holds `A` (PathNode), `B` (PathNode), `WallPoint`, `WallNormal`, `bOneWay`. In `ModMind.NextLeg`, after `FindPathToward` fills `RouteCache`, scan consecutive route nodes; if `RouteCache[i]==A && RouteCache[j]==B` with `j-i >= 2` (the walk route detours) **or** if the leg goal is on the other side of a link whose straight-line ratio beats the route by `> 2×`, replace that leg with the leap sequence: walk to `A` → `DoJumpTo(WallPoint)` → wall plant → `RequestLeapOffWall`-style second leap to `B` (or straight at the prey if in `LeapAttack` range). Cost profile: the pack role decides (flanker: accept if the link's end is on the prey's far side; closer: accept only when the second leap can target the prey). Nothing native needed.
2. **Native special ReachSpec (later, L).** AdventNative constructs a `ReachSpec` (`StaticConstructObject(ReachSpec)`), fills `Start/End/Distance/reachFlags = R_SPECIAL|R_JUMP(?)/CollisionRadius/Height`, appends it to `A.PathList` and marks `B` as the end so the native search sees the link and the stock `SuggestMovePreparation` on a `ModLeapSpot extends JumpDest` performs the jump. Needs the native `reachFlags` values and the search's treatment of `R_SPECIAL` confirmed in Ghidra first; the payoff is that `FindPathTo` itself prefers wall routes.

**Automatic placement rules** (run once per level from the PathNode graph, results cached per map; mirror Doom 3's edge-overlap/height-band/clear-hull tests and Unity's edge-walk):
- **Rule 1 — wall-kick pair.** Nodes `A,B` with `|AB| <= 1.5 × LeapMaxRange` and route distance `>= 2.5 × |AB|` (or unreachable). Sample candidate wall points on the two sides of `AB`: trace from the segment midpoint sideways up to `0.6 × LeapMaxRange`; accept a hit whose normal satisfies `-0.17 <= n.z <= 0.5` (the stock `WallJumpBegin` band) and whose plant height is `>= 1 × CollisionHeight` above `A`'s floor and `<= JumpZ²/(2|g|)` above it; then both arcs `A→W` and `W→B` must pass a hull trace (pawn extent) along the ballistic curve (Doom 3's `TestTrajectory`: 2–4 segments). Store `W,n`. One-way unless the reverse also passes.
- **Rule 2 — ledge/overhang vault.** `B.Z - A.Z` between step height and `JumpZ²/(2|g|) - 16`, horizontal `|AB_xy| <= GroundSpeed × flight time`, and the vertical hull trace down onto `B`'s floor edge is clear (Doom 3 `BarrierJump`); this is just a `JumpDest` the editor did not build — place a `ModLeapSpot` there.
- **Rule 3 — ceiling / pipe span (only after (c) is proven).** Find a surface with `n.z < -0.5` (ceiling) or a pipe/beam static mesh; entry node `A` under one end, exit node `B` under the other, both within leap reach of the surface; sample the span every `CollisionRadius`: the surface must be within `CollisionRadius + 20` along `-n` and the sample sphere must be clear of blocking actors. Store the sample polyline as the crawl path.

### (b) The leap itself (S–M)
`PHYS_Falling` with the native solve: `DoJumpTo(W)` already does the ballistic velocity (ModAction's vault solve is the script backup). On `NotifyHitWall`/the falling trace (reuse Seeker's `CheckWallJump` logic in `ModMind` since `SeekerDog` lacks it): call `EonPawn.WallJumpBegin(norm)` (a plain function, callable from script) **if** the dog mesh has `WallJump_L/R`; otherwise hold a wall-plant pose for ~0.15 s by the bone callback (Hips rotated so the dog's belly faces `-n`, legs under it, i.e. the orient-to-floor code pointed at the wall) with `SetPhysics(PHYS_None)` and `Velocity=0`, then `SetPhysics(PHYS_Falling)` + `DoLeapAttackTo(prey)` / `DoJumpTo(B)`. Launch on a frame event, not state entry (Doom 3 `AI_JUMP`), so the pose reads.

### (c) Ceiling / pipe crawling (M, gated by a 1-hour test)
Test first: pilot command that puts a hound at a ceiling point with `SetPhysics(PHYS_Spider)` and logs `Physics`, `Floor`, `Location` over 2 s while `MoveTo` is issued along the ceiling. If the native keeps `PHYS_Spider` and updates `Floor`, use it: crawl = `MoveTo` along the Rule-3 polyline with the bone callback rotating Hips to `-Floor` and foot IK tracing along `-Floor`; exit by `DoJump` (which already launches along `Floor`) or `DoLeapAttackTo`. If the native drops to `PHYS_Falling` or refuses, fake it: `PHYS_Flying` (stock Bot uses it) or `PHYS_None` with per-tick `SetLocation` along the polyline, rotation from the normal, same bone callback. Either way the AI side is identical: a `ModLeapLink` with a crawl polyline between its two leaps.

### (d) Which creatures, and the pack roles (S once (a)+(b) exist)
- **Hounds (quadrupeds):** the RAGE hopper. Closer role: `TryLeapAttack` → wall-kick when the direct lunge is blocked or the prey is pinned against a wall (kick off the wall beside the prey, land on it). Flanker: takes a Rule-1/Rule-3 link whose end is behind the prey; its `ExtraCost` profile should penalise the floor route (already supported by NextLeg cost profiles). Skip role: no change.
- **Seekers:** already own the chain; raise `WallJumpAbility` under the right psychology (aggressive/elite), and let the flanker Seeker use Rule-1 links as routes rather than only as attack flourishes.
- Keep ceiling crawl to hounds and the small Seeker variants (Scanner); big bipeds on ceilings read wrong.

## 5. Papers and code on automatic link generation (licences)

- **Recast & Detour** (Mikko Mononen et al.), zlib licence. Off-mesh connections are user-supplied (`dtOffMeshConnection`: two endpoints, radius, bidirectional flag, user id/flags/area); Recast itself does not auto-generate them. https://github.com/recastnavigation/recastnavigation **[D]**
- **Unity** auto Drop-Down / Jump-Across links (edge walk + landing-on-mesh + clear trajectory; parameters Drop Height, Jump Distance). Docs only, proprietary engine; the rule is what matters. https://docs.unity3d.com/2023.1/Documentation/Manual/nav-BuildingOffMeshLinksAutomatically.html **[D]**
- **Unreal Engine 5.5+** "Automatic Navigation Link Generation" (jump-down links sampled per border edge; `GeneratedNavLinksProxy` for custom traversal). Epic source licence (reference only). https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/automatic-navigation-link-generation **[D]**
- **P.C. van de Kerkhof, "Navigating Jumping Agents Through Virtual Environments"**, MSc thesis, Utrecht University (supervisor R.J. Geraerts), 2016, CC BY-NC-ND. Fully automatic jump-link generation on any triangle soup, implemented as a Recast extension, "jump links for large, complex environments within seconds", plus path planning over meshes with jump links. https://studenttheses.uu.nl/handle/20.500.12932/22818 **[D]** Companion thesis "Mesh Navigation Through Jumping" (Utrecht) on minimal/optimal jumps between surface points.
- **PathEngine** `autoGenerateConnections` — sample points around mesh edges + a callback deciding whether a connection is possible. Commercial; API doc only. https://pathengine.com/Contents/APIReference/Interfaces/iMesh/autoGenerateConnections/page.php **[D]**
- **Doom 3 `AASReach.cpp`** (GPL v3) and **Quake III `be_aas_reach.c`** (GPL v2): the oldest open generators of step/barrier-jump/walk-off-ledge/ladder/jumppad reachabilities; the edge-plane-overlap test is the one to copy in spirit. **[D]**
- **Ben Sunshine-Hill (Havok)**: arc-sweep per jump animation to auto-place vault/jump links (PC Gamer coverage) and GDC 2022 "Down with Nav(mesh)". **[D]**
- **Michael Booth, "The AI Systems of Left 4 Dead"** (Valve, AIIDE 2009): link-free algorithmic climbing by hull traces + nearest-height climb clip. Slides are Valve's; ideas only. **[D]**
- Not consulted (members-only or not found): GDC Vault talks by Berteling (Horizon 2018), Campbell (DOOM 2017), McIntosh (TLOU 2014), Techland's Kulon (GDC 2015 Dying Light movement).

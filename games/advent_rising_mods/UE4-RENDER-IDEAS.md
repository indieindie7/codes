# UE4 (4.27) render ideas for a D3D9 post wrapper (own words, no code)

Source read: H:\re\ue4-ref (sparse partial clone, 41 MB incl. .git). Paths relative to Engine/Shaders/Private unless noted. Ideas only; nothing copied.
UnrealTournament repo: only the old UE4-era shader set (ACES, Atmosphere); nothing custom worth noting.

## Ranked techniques
1. Depth-only screen-space contact shadows (DeferredLightingCommon.ush, ShadowRayCast; length set in screen or world space). A short ray march from each pixel toward the light, tested against the depth buffer, darkens small crevices and character-to-floor contact. Feasible: YES. World normals not needed, the ray only needs depth plus the light direction (a guessed sun/key-light vector). ps_3_0 limit means a fixed 8-16 step loop. Cost low-medium. Adds grounded feet, weapon-on-wall and rubble shadowing under the PCSS shadows.
2. Exponential height fog with sky/sun inscatter tint (HeightFogCommon.ush: GetExponentialHeightFog, CalculateHeightFog; HeightFogPixelShader.usf). Closed-form integral of a density that falls off with height along the view ray, plus a directional glow toward the sun. Feasible: YES, depth gives view distance, camera height/ray direction rebuilt from the inverse projection. Very cheap. Adds layered low mist in corridors and alien landscapes; a better base for our sun shafts.
3. Camera and per-object motion blur with depth-aware tile blending (PostProcessMotionBlur.usf, MainMotionBlurCommon, SampleWeight/DepthCmp/SpreadCmp; PostProcessVelocityFlatten.usf). Blur along velocity, weighting samples by depth and spread so fast foreground does not smear background. Feasible: PARTLY. Camera-only blur from depth plus previous view matrix is easy; per-object velocity needs a velocity MRT, only for characters (we may write one). Cost medium. Adds speed on dashes/powers.
4. Temporal accumulation done with neighbourhood clamp and velocity dilation (TemporalAA/TAAStandalone.usf, TAAUpdateHistory.usf, TAADilateVelocity.usf, TAAFilterFrequencies.usf). Even with SMAA, a jittered projection plus clamped history kills shimmer on thin cables, grates, hair. Feasible: PARTLY. We cannot jitter the game's projection reliably (possible by patching the projection constant), and no depth when MSAA is on. Cost medium. Would help specular shimmer. Risky; try last.
5. Filmic tonemapper with exposed toe/shoulder/slope and shadow tint (TonemapCommon.ush: FilmPostProcess, constants FilmSlope/FilmToe/FilmShoulder; PostProcessTonemap.usf, TonemapCommonPS; ACES.usf in 4.x). Our grading presets are LDR curves; a parametrised S-curve with a colour-matrix and shadow/highlight tint gives cinematic roll-off. Feasible: YES on an LDR input too, though without HDR the shoulder only compresses already clipped values. Cost trivial. Adds mood grading for dark scenes.
6. Eye adaptation / auto exposure from log-average luminance (PostProcessEyeAdaptation.usf, BasicEyeAdaptation; PostProcessHistogram.usf for the histogram variant). Feasible: YES via a chain of downsample passes to 1x1 (no compute), smoothed over time, applied as a gentle exposure gain with min/max clamp. Cost low. Adds the dark-room to bright-door effect and punch from muzzle flashes. Keep range small since input is LDR.
7. Gather-based depth of field with a circle-of-confusion pass and foreground/background split (DiaphragmDOF/ folder: DOFSetup.usf, DOFGatherPass.usf, DOFRecombine.usf, DOFCocTileDilate.usf; older PostProcessDOF.usf). Feasible: YES for a simple version: CoC from depth, half-res two-layer gather with Poisson/spiral taps and bokeh-weighted brightness. Cost medium. Use only in cutscenes or aim-down-sights focus; our depth cannot be read under MSAA, so use the resolved depth copy.
8. Screen-space reflections with hierarchical depth ray march and roughness fade (SSRT/SSRTRayCast.ush: CastScreenSpaceRay, ComputeHitVignetteFromScreenPos; SSRT/SSRTReflections.usf, GetRoughnessFade). Feasible: PARTLY. Needs a normal to reflect: only characters have it, and flat floors could use a fixed up-vector guess (wet or metal floors via a user mask or a height heuristic). ps_3_0 loops of 24-32 steps are OK. Cost medium-high. Adds sheen on polished sci-fi floors; wrong on non-flat world surfaces, so restrict to near-up reconstructed normals from depth derivatives.
9. Chromatic aberration, vignette, film grain, sharpen all in the final pass (PostProcessTonemap.usf, params ChromaticAberrationParams, GrainFromUV, TonemapperParams x=vignette y=sharpen). Cheap polish. Grain is temporally jittered hash noise; fringe scales with distance from centre. Feasible: YES, trivial. Adds lens character for hits and damage states (drive fringe with damage).
10. Lens flare and bloom variants: threshold plus ghost/halo flare (PostProcessLensFlares.usf, PostProcessBloom.usf, BloomSetup; PostProcessFFTBloom.usf for convolution bloom with a custom kernel texture). Feasible: flare YES (downsampled bright pass, mirrored ghost taps, radial halo, chromatic split); FFT bloom NO (needs compute). A cheaper stand-in is a multi-scale bloom pyramid with weights per level, which gives the long-tail look without FFT. Cost low. Adds energy-weapon glare.

## Where UE4 beats our version
- SSAO: UE4 ships GTAO (PostProcessAmbientOcclusion.usf, GTAO_* defines, thickness heuristic, 4-tap smooth pass, temporal history). The horizon-based cosine-weighted integral with a thickness heuristic avoids halo and over-darkening of thin objects that plain obscurance gives, and adds multi-bounce colour-bleed brightening in light albedo. The thickness idea and the temporal accumulation are portable; the horizon search needs a normal, which we can rebuild from depth derivatives at ps_3_0.
- Subsurface: UE4 offers the Burley normalised diffusion profile (SubsurfaceBurleyNormalized.ush, SubsurfaceProfileCommon.ush) with per-material profile kernels, a boundary colour-bleed term and a depth-in-alpha mask. A single-pass profile with a per-channel radius (red wider than blue) approximates it in our existing separable blur.
- Screen GI: SSGI (SSRT/SSRTDiffuseIndirect.usf) reuses the previous frame's colour through a HZB ray march and then denoises (ScreenSpaceDenoise/, SSDSpatialAccumulation.usf, SSDTemporalAccumulation.usf). The takeaway is the denoiser design: spatial kernel guided by depth/normal, then a temporal blend with history rejection. Our cascades could borrow that temporal reject step.
- Shadows: capsule shadows (CapsuleShadowShaders.usf) give cheap soft indirect occlusion under characters from simple capsule proxies; we could fake one with a blurred dark ellipse projected at each character's feet. Distance-field AO (DistanceFieldAmbientOcclusion.cpp): no, needs scene SDFs.
- Tonemap pipeline works in HDR then a baked 3D LUT (PostProcessCombineLUTs.usf, ColorLookupTable in the tonemap). Our colour grading as a 32^3 LUT (stored as a 1024x32 strip, ps_3_0 friendly) would unify the presets, colourblind filters and fades in one cheap fetch.
- Volumetric fog (VolumetricFog.usf) uses a froxel grid with compute; not portable. Approximate with height fog (item 2) plus the sun-shaft pass.
- Hair (HairBsdf.ush, HairShadingCommon.ush): dual-lobe Marschner-style highlight with shifted tangents. We lack tangents; only worth a fake via screen-derivative direction.

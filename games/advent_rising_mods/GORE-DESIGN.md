# Advent Rising gore system: design

The gore and blood system of AdventMod, the Advent Rising (Unreal Engine 2, 2005) mod. It covers what it does, how the parts fit together, where the code is, how to tune it, and how it was tested.

Status (2026-10-08): every feature below is built and has been checked in hidden test runs in the game. The person-in-the-loop playtest, the in-game menu switches and release packaging are still to do (see [Still to do](#still-to-do)).

---

## 1. Goals

- **Violence that reads and teaches.** A hit should say where it landed and how hard; a kill should leave a scene that tells what happened.
- **Blood behaves like a liquid.** It sprays, pools, runs down walls and bodies under gravity, drips off ledges, stretches into strings, gets walked through, and dries.
- **It never costs a frame.** Fights stay at the 60 fps cap with no hitches you can feel.
- **Our own art only.** Every texture is generated by our scripts (`tools/make_*.py`) or drawn procedurally in a shader. No game pixels are copied or shipped.
- **Everything has an off switch and a budget.** Caps on decals, drops, strings and sources keep long fights stable.

## 2. Architecture

Three layers, each doing what it is good at:

```
 UnrealScript (AdventMod.u)            native bridge                 d3d8 layer (d3d8.dll, our d3d8to9 fork)
 ─────────────────────────────         ─────────────────             ─────────────────────────────────────────
 game events: hits, kills, gibs,  ──►  AdventNative.dll        ──►   U2BloodCommand(): "run", "drip", "streak",
 severs, footsteps, wounds             NativeCall("Blood:...")      "string", "hands", "lens", "pool"...
 decals (projectors), gib meshes,      (commands under 256 chars)    world-space effects the engine can't do:
 drops, footprints, coats, ragdolls                                  render-to-texture sheets, extra shader passes
                                                                     on characters, post-process passes
```

- **Script** owns gameplay truth: who was hit, where, how hard, which bone, and which blood (red humans, purple aliens). It places decals and actors the engine already knows how to draw.
- **The d3d8 layer** draws what the 2003 engine can't. UE2 projectors on skinned meshes smear into one colour, and its decals can't animate, so the layer adds:
  - textures painted live (pools, wall runs);
  - extra shader passes over the game's own character draws (body streaks, blood on hands);
  - geometry of its own (goo strings);
  - post passes (the wet lens).
- **AdventNative.dll** is the bridge. Script calls `NativeCall("Blood:<command>")`; the DLL forwards it to the d3d8 layer's exported `U2BloodCommand`.
- **Blood kinds** are used everywhere: `BloodKind(Pawn)` gives 0 none (robots, vehicles), 1 red (humans), 2 alien purple (Seekers and other aliens).

## 3. Features

### 3.1 Hits and reactions
- **Flinch and stagger** at the hit bone, plus front and back knockdowns and get-ups (`ModReact.uc`).
- **Death animations and Karma ragdolls:** a ragdoll profile of our own, then the ragdoll freezes once at rest (`ModDeathAnims.uc`, `ModRagdollBones.uc`).
- **Hounds:** baked knockdown and front-death clips (`ModHoundAnims.uc`, `tools/make_hound_clips.py`).
- **Wounds:** a lump of meat (`ModStump`) riding the bone nearest the hit, up to `WoundsPerBody`. Some wounds become **spurts** that pulse in heartbeats for a few seconds and paint what's near.
- **Bleeding:** the badly wounded leave a trail. A wound clots over `BleedClot` seconds.

### 3.2 Decals: sprays, splats, pools (`ModGore.Mark`, `ModBloodDecal.uc`)
- **Sprays** behind the victim along the shot, and **splats** under the hit, scaled by damage. The budget is `MaxDecals` (80) marks, oldest first out.
- **Live pools:** a pool is a render-to-texture sheet the d3d8 layer fills over time (`blood.hpp`). It grows from a body and can be poured into by drops.
- **Gloss and drying:** every blood decal gets a second shader pass (`blood_gloss.hlsl`, `gloss=` rules).
  - Wet blood has a specular sheen and a Fresnel rim.
  - It dries over `glossdry` (60–240 s): darker, browner, matte.
  - Deep pools carry a **screen-space reflection** of the room (`glossreflect`).

### 3.3 Wall runs (`runs.hpp`, `ModGore.WallRun`)
- Blood that lands on a wall (|N.Z| < 0.5) opens a **run region**: a 128×128 live sheet in a 150-unit decal.
- Drops with mass flow "down" the wall in texture space:
  - they slow as they leave their trail and wobble sideways;
  - heavy ones sometimes branch;
  - each ends in a bead.
- A run plays out over about 10 seconds. The trail is laid across even lanes as wide as the drop.
- Tuned offline first in `tools/runs_sim.py`, which draws the same simulation as a time strip.
- Settings: `RunSize`, `RunChance`, `bWallRuns`. Gib bursts start wall runs too.

### 3.4 Blood streaks on bodies (`streaks.hpp`, `ModGore`)
- Each bleeding point (wounds, hits on corpses, cuts) is a **streak source** sent every tick (at most 8).
- After each character draw, the layer draws the character again with a streak shader:
  - 2–4 thin rivulets run **straight down in world space** from each source;
  - they grow with age, end in a drip bead, and darken as they dry;
  - they take the scene's lighting and fog.
- Only geometry within about 25 units of a source is painted, so the streaks stay on the wounded body.
- World position per pixel comes from fixed-function camera-space texgen plus the inverse view.
- Settings: `streaks=`, `streakparams=` (width, length, speed, reach), `streakfx=`.

### 3.5 Goo strings (`strings.hpp`, `ModGore`, `ModSever`)
- **Where they come from:**
  - a severed limb gets a strand back to its stump;
  - a gib gets 1–3 strands between neighbouring pieces;
  - at most 12 at once.
- **Shape:** a camera-facing ribbon of about 16 segments that sags under gravity and thins in the middle as it stretches (volume kept). It is drawn with the game's depth, so walls hide it.
- **Snapping:**
  - a string holds for `GooHold` (0.5–1.8 s), then snaps past rest × `GooStretch` (2.5–5);
  - a hard over-stretch (4× the limit) snaps it at once;
  - snapping leaves two shortening, dripping halves and a splat below.
- While stretched it pulls on the flying piece (`GooPull`).
- Its width never drops below 1.2 px, so it doesn't break into dots far away.

### 3.6 Blood coat (`ModBloodCoat.uc`)
- A bloodied body's skins become Combiners: its own texture × a blood texture (the coat).
- Fresh hits pan drip streaks across the coat for a few seconds.
- The player's coat wears off over time.

### 3.7 Falling drops (`ModBloodDrop.uc`, `ModGore`)
- **Where drops come from:**
  - wounds (easing off as they clot);
  - dead bodies with air below them;
  - the lower edge of wall sprays;
  - snapped goo strings;
  - **any blood on a ceiling or overhang**, every 1.5–4 s for 20 s.
- Drops are sprites with gravity and a landing trace each tick. They stretch into streaks when falling fast.
- A landing drop pours into a live pool if one is there, otherwise it leaves a tiny splat.
- At most `MaxDrops` (24) are in the air, pooled and reused. Art: `tools/make_blood_drops.py`.

### 3.8 Bloody footprints (`ModGore.WetFeetScan`, `PrintAt`)
- **What wets the feet** (the player and NPCs alike):
  - fresh floor blood: splats, sprays and pools from the last `FootFresh` (90 s);
  - standing next to a body.
- Prints are laid every `FootStride` (45) units, longer when running, alternating left and right along the walking direction.
- They fade over `FootprintSteps` (10). At most `MaxFootprints` (40) exist, inside the decal budget.

### 3.9 Blood on the player's hands and weapon (`ModPlayerBlood.uc`, `streaks.hpp`)
- **What raises it:** kills, gibs and punches up close, and blood landing on the player.
- **How it fades:** dark after about 45 s, gone after 2–3 minutes.
- Hands, forearms and the held weapon are sent as up to 5 capsules. The layer paints a smear pattern along each limb's own axes, so it moves with the arm.
- Settings: `hands=`, `handsparams=`.

### 3.10 Wet lens (`lens.hpp`, `ModPlayerBlood.uc`)
- **What happens:** blood landing within `LensNear` (120 units) of the camera puts up to 6 drops on the "lens", after the final post pass.
- **How they look:** drops refract the picture behind them, with a faint red or purple rim. They slide down and fade over about 4.5 s.
- **The old splats:** the drops replace the old HUD splats (`ModScreenBlood`), which come back if `lens=0`.
- Settings: `lens=`, `lensparams=`, `lensfx=`. It needs `post=1`.

### 3.11 Gibs and severing (`ModGib*.uc`, `ModSever.uc`)
- **Gibs:** pieces with physics; a burst of blood all round (it starts wall runs too); body-part cards drawn from the victim's own look (`ModGibAtlas`: one ScriptedTexture per slot).
- **Severing:** limbs come off at named cuts, leaving a stump, a thrown piece and goo strings.

## 4. Performance

- **Shader compile cache:** every shader source compiles once, and all of them compile at level load (about 0.4 s), including the streak and string shaders. Before this, each new blood texture appearing mid-fight compiled its own copy of the same shader: 100–200 ms hitches.

  | heavy fight window | frames > 50 ms | worst frame | 1% low |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | before | 10 | 283 ms | 49.8 fps |
  | after | 0–1 | 54 ms | 56 fps |

- **The live-ini watch** reloads rules only when a rule line changes. Runtime switches such as `pcss=` used to recompile everything, which caused the 1.3 s freezes in the user's recording.
- **The indoor/outdoor shadow switch** judges by 5 points with a 4 s hysteresis, so it doesn't flip under beams.
- **Budgets:** 80 decals, 48 wounds, 24 drops, 12 strings, 8 streak sources, 5 hand capsules, 6 lens drops.
- **Measuring:** `perf.hpp` writes a line to U2Shaders.log every 30 s (avg fps, 1% low, worst frame, frames over 50 ms) and logs each hitch over 80 ms with a tick stamp. AdventNative.log lines carry the same stamp, so hitches can be matched to gore events.

## 5. Settings

**`AdventMod.ini`**, section `[AdventMod.ModGore]` (main keys):
- `bBlood`, `bWounds`, `bBleedTrail`, `BleedClot`, `MaxDecals`, `MaxWounds`, `WoundsPerBody`
- `bWallRuns`, `RunSize`, `RunChance`
- `bGooStrings`, `GooRest*`, `GooStretch*`, `GooHold*`, `GooLife*`, `GooThick`, `GooPull`
- `bDrips`, `DripRate`, `MaxDrops`, `DropSize`
- `bFootprints`, `FootprintSteps`, `MaxFootprints`, `FootFresh`, `FootStride`

**`AdventMod.ini`**, section `[AdventMod.ModPlayerBlood]`: `bHands`, `bLens`, `bReplaceScreenBlood`, `FadeSecs`, `DrySecs`, `LensNear`.

**`System\U2Shaders.ini`** (d3d8 layer; reloads live while the game runs):
- `gloss=`, `glossfx=`, `glossenv=`, `glossdry=`, `glossreflect=`
- `bloodrun=`
- `streaks=`, `streakparams=`, `streakfx=`
- `strings=`, `stringparams=`, `stringfx=`
- `hands=`, `handsparams=`
- `lens=`, `lensparams=`, `lensfx=`

## 6. Testing

- **Hidden test runs:** `test_run.ps1` with ModPilot steps, run in an off-screen window, with prints (`shotp`).
- **Gore steps:**
  - `HURT`, `SPAWN`, `GIBAHEAD`, `GOOSEVER [cut]`, `GOOLIST`
  - `DRIPTEST`, `DROPLIST`, `WALKBLOOD dist kind`, `STEPLIST`
  - `bGoreLog=True` logs every decision.
- **Offline simulators** (draw the effect without the game):
  - `tools/runs_sim.py` (wall runs)
  - `tools/streaks_sim.py` (body streaks)
  - `tools/strings_sim.py` (goo)
  - `tools/lens_sim.py` (lens)
- **Measurements:**
  - `tools/python/visualqa/blood_drying.py` measures drying from prints (brightness, redness, gloss over time);
  - `perf:` lines give frame times.
- **Seen in game** (hidden test runs, 2026-10-07/08): wall runs over 10 s; streaks on a wounded soldier; purple alien runs, streaks and coats on level03sectionc; goo strings between gib pieces; drops (21 thrown, 21 landed); footprints (alternating prints logged); hands and lens on a point-blank gib; the FPS before/after above.

## 7. Rules kept

- No game pixels in git, docs, video or release zips. Effects are procedural or use generated textures.
- Mods are GPL and non-commercial.
- The FPS graph never ships enabled.
- Every effect has an off switch, and the d3d8 code defaults to off; the mod's shipped ini turns features on.

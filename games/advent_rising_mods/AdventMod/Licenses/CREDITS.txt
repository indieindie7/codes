  https://iquilezles.org/articles/texturerepetition/

- Techniques followed, in our own code: hex-tiling after Morten S. Mikkelsen
  (2022) in terrain_src.hlsl; Scalable Ambient Obscurance after McGuire, Mara
  and Luebke (2012) in ssao.hlsl; Separable Subsurface Scattering after
  Jimenez et al. (2015) in sss.hlsl (our own kernel); radiance cascades after
  Alexander Sannikov in gi.hlsl; Valve's light-warp ramp and Penner's
  pre-integrated skin shading in char_skin.hlsl; NVIDIA's PCSS (Fernando
  2005) in pcss_proj.hlsl.

- Bloom down/up sampling after Jorge Jimenez, "Next Generation Post Processing
  in Call of Duty: Advanced Warfare" (SIGGRAPH 2014).

- Death animations: clips generated with NVIDIA Kimodo (its code is
  Apache-2.0; the models under the NVIDIA Open Model licence), then edited;
  the blade swings and the hound clips are hand-keyed. Only the motion data
  ships, none of Kimodo.

Advent Rising is (c) GlyphX Games / Majesco. No game files and no game
textures are included. A few meshes are derived from the game's own models

and the armour data files: geometry plus one armour bit per face, no pixels).
